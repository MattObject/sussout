# Project Document: The Sanctuary Initiative

## Project Vision
A fantasy environment designed as a restorative sanctuary for individuals seeking escape from the reactionary social dynamics of the "old world." The setting is established in the aftermath of a collapsed fascist regime, aiming to create a stable, meritocratic society built upon the psychological and intellectual rehabilitation of those previously influenced by reactionary ideologies.

## Core Features

### 1. Social & Governance Structure
*   **Bottom-Up Meritocracy:** Power and influence are derived from individual capability and demonstrated merit rather than inherited status or top-down decree.
*   **Post-Regime Transition:** The previous fascist hierarchy has been dismantled; leaders and high-level collaborators have been removed via legal action (imprisonment) or death (following the leader's final command).

### 2. The Dechudification Program
A structured, rehabilitative pathway for individuals identified as "chuds" (uninformed, reactionary individuals from the previous era) to reintegrate into the community.
*   **Therapeutic Rehabilitation:** Daily empathy-building exercises and therapeutic interventions designed to dismantle reactionary cognitive patterns.
*   **Progressive Pedagogy:** An educational framework that begins with simplified, accessible connection points and gradually advances toward complex critical thinking skills.
*   **Relapse Protocol:** Any regression into reactionary thought patterns triggers mandatory re-enrollment in intensified dechudification tracks.

### 3. Border Control & Isolation
*   **Geographical Containment:** The community is physically isolated from the outside world to prevent "ideological contagion."
*   **Prefecture Gates:** Controlled entry points where all individuals are screened for reactionary tendencies.
*   **Policy of Expulsion:** Individuals who refuse to participate in the dechudification process are denied entry and permanently expelled from the sanctuary.

### 4. Economic Model
*   **Incentivized Compliance:** The community offers a high standard of living and "comforts" with minimal labor requirements for those who have successfully completed the dechudification process.
*   **Labor-Light Lifestyle:** The economic incentive is structured to encourage voluntary rehabilitation by linking social ease to ideological alignment.

## Key Decisions
*   **Status of the "Chud":** Reached a consensus that "chud" identity is treated as a rehabilitatable state rather than an inherent or permanent trait.
*   **Enforcement Mechanism:** Decided on physical/geographical isolation and expulsion as the primary tool for maintaining social integrity, rather than internal policing.
*   **Economic Lever:** Used post-labor comfort as the primary motivator for individuals to undergo the pedagogical transition.

## Identified Assumptions & Future Considerations
*   **Infrastructure Sustainability:** The challenge of maintaining the physical infrastructure (gates, educational systems, etc.) in an economy that de-emphasizes traditional labor.
*   **Merit Definition:** The ongoing need to define "merit" in a way that prevents the emergence of new, exclusionary hierarchies.
*   **Contagion Management:** Developing robust methods for detecting subtle ideological leakage from outside territories despite physical barriers.
